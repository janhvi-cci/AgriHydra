Polyhouse Questions, Answered

Which polyhouse type is right for my region?+

It depends on your climate, crop and budget — our site survey team recommends NVPH, fan-pad, shade net or a hybrid design after assessing your land and local weather data.

What is the typical cost per acre?+

Turnkey costs are illustratively around ₹18,00,000 per acre before subsidy, varying by structure type, cladding grade and automation level. Use our ROI calculator for a personalized estimate.

Do you handle the subsidy application for polyhouses?+

Yes — our subsidy desk manages eligibility checks, documentation and liaison with central and state horticulture missions, typically covering up to 60% of project cost.

What maintenance does a polyhouse need?+

Routine checks on cladding tension, vent motors, fogging nozzles and structural fasteners. We offer an optional Annual Maintenance Contract to handle this for you.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply

These schemes are open to a wide range of applicants, not just individual landowners.

Individual Farmers

Landowning & tenant/lease-holding farmers with valid land records.

FPOs

Farmer Producer Organisations registered under Companies Act or Co-operative Societies Act.

SHGs

Registered Self-Help Groups engaged in agriculture or allied horticultural activity.

Registered Societies / Cooperatives

Cooperative societies and registered trusts undertaking horticulture or irrigation projects.

Agriculture Entrepreneurs

Private individuals or firms setting up commercial protected-cultivation or agri-infrastructure ventures.

Horticulture Growers

Growers of fruits, vegetables, spices, flowers or plantation crops expanding under protected cultivation.

Application Channels

Where to Apply

You can apply directly through any of these official government channels — or let our subsidy desk manage it for you.

District Agriculture Office

Your first point of contact for scheme information, application forms and field-level verification in your district.

District Horticulture Office

Handles MIDH, polyhouse and horticulture-specific subsidy applications, site inspection and disbursal tracking.

Each state runs its own horticulture/agriculture department portal for scheme-wise online applications and status tracking.
